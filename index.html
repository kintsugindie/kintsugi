A Note from the Workbench

金継

Independent & Self-Taught

Built after hours, fueled by tea & curiosity

Hi, I’m a solo hobbyist developer building software and small games in my spare time. Kintsugi Indie isn’t a company, a studio, or a startup chasing metrics—it’s simply a personal workshop where I turn late-night sketches into working things.

When you build alone without corporate deadlines, every bug, scrapped prototype, and rough edge becomes part of the learning process. Instead of hiding those seams, I like treating each project with care—fixing what breaks and keeping things simple, lightweight, and human.

Why “Kintsugi”?

“In kintsugi, cracks aren’t disguised—they are illuminated with gold, becoming the most honest part of the object’s history.”

Independent making is full of false starts, rough drafts, and small imperfections. Choosing the name Kintsugi Indie is a reminder that software and games don’t need to be massive or flawless to be meaningful—they just need care, honesty, and heart.
