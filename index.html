Self-Editing Architecture

How to Customize Tabs & Content

Every tab, project card, music track, digital storefront, and privacy policy clause is controlled by a single SITE_CONFIG object inside index.html.

  • Rename Brand Subtitle: Edit SITE_CONFIG.brand.tagline (e.g. Creative Atelier & Solo Production, Independent Studio, or Multi-Disciplinary Lab).
  • Add / Reorder Main Bar Tabs: Edit the SITE_CONFIG.navigation array. You can hide, rename, or add custom sections.
  • Add Apps, Games, Tracks & 3D/Stock Assets: Copy any item block inside apps, games, music, or digitalProducts and paste your real store URLs.
  • Paste Your Exact Privacy Policy: Update SITE_CONFIG.privacyPolicy.sections with your existing policy text anytime.
